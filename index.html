Stagger Coffee — Concept & work plan

The Stagger app.

One place for the café's day-to-day life: what's on, how busy it is, what to drink, what to buy, and points for being a regular. Below is what it would look like and how we'd build it in stages.

Concept only — every event, article, number and partner shown is sample content

01 — Scope

What's in, what's not.

A companion app, not an ordering app. Stagger stays a place you walk into, and tipping the team stays at the counter.

In the app

  • Store hours & live crowd meter with wait time
  • Events & workshops, with reminders
  • Menu & special drinks
  • Blog & stories
  • Store: coffee, matcha, clothing, bags, objects
  • Points: earn at the café, spend in the store

Extras that keep people coming back

  • Small games that earn a few points a day
  • Community partners & sponsors
  • Notifications for events & drops
  • Shipping & order tracking for store items

Not in this build

  • Pickup or drink ordering
  • Replacing the counter register
  • Table service

02 — How it looks

Eight screens.

Same cream, oat and matcha world as the website, with the dark matcha look for the store and games.

03 — Roadmap

Four stages, each shippable.

Each stage ends with something the café can use, so the app is useful early and grows. Durations are rough planning estimates that assume the content, prices and rules are confirmed up front, and will be firmed up once we start.

Foundation

Weeks 1–2 · Setup

  • Confirm hours, menu, prices and brand files
  • Decide points rules with their accountant
  • Pick tools: payments, shipping, content editor
  • Final designs for all eight screens

You get: signed-off designs and a fixed scope.

Front door

Weeks 3–8 · v1

  • Home, hours and directions
  • Crowd meter (one tap for the team)
  • Menu & special drinks
  • Events with reminders, blog
  • Simple editor so staff update content themselves

You get: an app on iPhone and Android that keeps regulars informed.

Regulars

Weeks 9–14 · v2

  • Accounts and sign-in
  • Points: scan at the counter to earn
  • Push notifications for events and specials
  • Cashier scanning tool

You get: a loyalty programme that works at the register.

Shop & play

Weeks 15–22 · v3

  • Store: coffee, matcha, clothing, bags
  • Checkout with cards, wallets and points
  • Shipping, taxes and order tracking
  • Games and sponsor placements

You get: the full app, with the store running inside it.

How the crowd meter works

  • Start simple. A barista taps Quiet, Steady, Busy or Packed on a phone or tablet.
  • Wait time is a range tied to each level, agreed with the team.
  • Later. It can be suggested automatically from register activity or a door counter, if they want that.

Points, kept simple

  • Earn: visits (scan at the counter), events, a few points from games each day.
  • Spend: store items, and free drinks at the counter.
  • Start as rewards, not cash. Points that act like stored money can bring gift-card rules in California, so their accountant should confirm before launch.

What we need from Stagger

  • Final hours (our sources still disagree) and the full menu
  • What runs at the counter today, and whether it can scan codes
  • Who updates events, blog and specials day to day
  • Shipping rules: where, how fast, returns
  • Partners or sponsors they'd like to feature
